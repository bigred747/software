# Original Social Growth Workflow

## Safe operating order

1. Open **Richard Brummer SEO → Social Growth**.
2. Choose one published page or enter a specific manual topic.
3. Name the exact audience.
4. Add at least 80 characters of firsthand experience, measurement, research, comparison, warning, or editorial conclusion.
5. Select the goal, format, angle, and duration.
6. Confirm the same-site destination.
7. Mark realistic AI media only when that accurately describes the final asset.
8. Complete the rights and originality attestation.
9. Generate the review-only package.
10. Record the final media, music, product footage, captions, tags, links, disclosures, audience, and account settings inside the official platform before publishing.
11. Enter one consistent reporting window of views, watch time, shares, saves, clicks, and follows.

## High-volume queue

Select up to ten published pages and create up to thirty idea-only records. The queue is intentionally not publish-ready. Each idea must be expanded with original firsthand evidence and a rights attestation.

A safe volume target is one that the editorial team can review, film, caption, fact-check, disclose, and measure accurately. The plugin does not reward bulk posting without evidence.

## Trial and cross-platform testing

When the official account offers Trial Reels, the checklist reminds the publisher to test suitable content with non-followers. Cross-posting should be reviewed separately because the cover, caption, tags, link behavior, audience, and product eligibility can differ between Instagram and Facebook.

## Translation

The plugin does not perform machine translation. It asks the publisher to add and review closed captions and translations in the official platform. Product names, prices, warranties, measurements, legal terms, and affiliate disclosures require human review in every language.

## AI labels

The package includes a conditional reminder for realistic AI-generated or materially altered media. The final label decision must follow the current platform interface and policy.

## Measurement

The local performance score uses:

- Average watch time relative to campaign duration.
- Weighted engagement from likes, comments, shares, and saves.
- Link-click rate.
- Follow rate.

The score is an internal comparison tool, not a Meta, Google, or industry benchmark.
