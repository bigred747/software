== Description ==

Luxe Google Presence 1.1.0 keeps one public document for computer and mobile, prints the Amazon Associate disclosure and price disclaimer, and adds rel="sponsored" without changing Amazon URLs. It replaces the generic affiliate line, sends weight and size filters to a real department, and repairs image labels that are Amazon file names.

It does not buy traffic, auto-redirect to Amazon, write posts, or replace Score Repair, Product Scout, Product Integrity, or Rank Math.

95 means the required live checks passed. 100 means those checks passed and the official Associate sentence plus the full price disclaimer are on the page.
