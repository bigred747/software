    Candles paint green (up / bullish momentum) or red (down / bearish momentum) only — never gray or yellow. Desk ADX · DMI sets conviction; weak ADX still stays green/red. Ready · Aim · Fire marks Edge staging on the chart (R / A dots, then Fire BUY/SELL arrows at the next open when big4indicator agrees). Status chips above the chart read trend, breakdown or breakout, squeeze alert, Edge consensus, and VWAP from closed bars. Under price: true momentum (14/5/3) with a green/red main and signal cloud and cyan/pink cross arrows; then this desk's squeeze momentum histogram (cyan/blue up, yellow/red down) with compression dots; then CCI 50 (white), 14 (yellow), and 5 (cyan) with ±100 lines and green/red arrows on closed-bar crosses; then Volume Wave · Desk Waves — Hull ATR (n=6) × ATRmult (0.7) gates accum/distrib volume waves (green up / red down hist, cyan average); settings live on the pane; wave paint stays in the Volume Wave pane.       How Edge Fire works

      Edge buys when seven checks turn on together on closed bars: TMO above −10, TMO turns green (main above signal / bullish green paint), five squeeze dots or currently in a squeeze, squeeze momentum above zero, CCI 50 · 14 · 5 all into zone +100 on a closed-bar cross, Desk Waves green / up (Volume Wave pane under CCI — fail closed without volume), and VIX falling on its latest closed daily bar vs the prior close (real ^VIX / .VIX only — fail closed if missing; TICK/ADD/TRIN/VOLD stay No feed and are never invented). Ready at 5 of 7, Aim at 6, Fire at 7. The same Fire gates drive Squeeze scan, Fire 5, and other desk scanners. The fill is the next open. It sells at the next open when squeeze momentum drops below zero or the close breaks a trailing stop 2.5 average ranges under the best close. The record is this market's past bars with 10 basis points each way for the fee and slippage. It is not a forecast.
